## OptiScaler MFG source

`MfgUnlock.cpp` and `MfgUnlock.h` adapt the RTX 40 Ada implementation from
[`y4my4my4m/OptiScaler_DLSSNR_Multipass_MFG`](https://github.com/y4my4my4m/OptiScaler_DLSSNR_Multipass_MFG),
tag `v4` (`7b7220bb`), under GPL-3.0. Its reviewed signature and kernel work was
compared with [`evairx/OptiScaler-MFG`](https://github.com/evairx/OptiScaler-MFG),
release `dev_8`, commit `e69d6f87072e381a77f65b5d4852b7167b5d10d5`.

The upstream project is archived, and its RTX 40 patch is experimental. The
FrameBridge implementation is disabled by default, patches memory only, and
does not distribute a modified NVIDIA DLL.
